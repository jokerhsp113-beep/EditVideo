# Bundled typography
Patrick Hand Regular, from https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/patrickhand .
License: SIL Open Font License 1.1, included in OFL-PatrickHand.txt.
Full TTF includes Latin and Vietnamese; do not replace with a Latin-only subset.
Loaded locally through FontFace with a Remotion render barrier. Failure cancels rendering.
Only weight 400 exists; use size/spacing for hierarchy. Unsupported glyphs require review.

Noto Sans Regular (OFL): https://github.com/notofonts/noto-fonts/tree/main/hinted/ttf/NotoSans . Bundled fallback for symbols absent in handwriting; see OFL-NotoSans.txt.

Noto Sans Symbols 2 Regular (OFL): https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssymbols2 . Bundled for arrows and star glyphs.

Noto Sans Math Regular (OFL): https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosansmath . Arrow/mathematical glyph coverage; see OFL-NotoSansMath.txt.
